<p class="about-keywords">Terahertz photonics · High-speed communication testbeds · Circuit design</p>

I’m a Research Scientist at [TeraX Labs](https://web.spms.ntu.edu.sg/~ranjans/index.html), NTU, working under [Prof. Ranjan Singh](https://web.spms.ntu.edu.sg/~ranjans/prof.html). My recently submitted [Ph.D. thesis](https://hdl.handle.net/10356/205858) focused on terahertz photonics and communication testbed development, alongside contributions to terahertz electronic circuit design. I’m interested in how fundamental physics and engineering can advance next-generation wireless systems and ultra-high-speed data links.

**Currently:** thin-film lithium niobate modulators and waveguide-integrated superconducting nanowire single-photon detectors.
